Repair or replace?

The right answer depends on the condition of the existing structure, the extent of deterioration or damage, planned branding changes, desired life cycle and the cost of continuing to repair isolated systems. A documented condition review gives owners a better basis for comparing those options.

Aging Structure

Extensive deterioration, recurring steel repairs or widespread corrosion may point toward replacement.

Major Reimage

A full site redevelopment can be the right time to replace obsolete canopy systems rather than rebuild around them.

Repeated Failures

Ongoing drainage, decking, fascia and framing issues can make piecemeal repair increasingly inefficient.

Replacement should solve more than one problem.

A replacement project can address structural condition, clearance, drainage, fascia, lighting and appearance at the same time. Lake James coordinates those systems as a complete canopy project.

If the existing canopy may still be repairable, start with a documented inspection or review our repair capabilities.
