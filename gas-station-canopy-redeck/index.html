Decking is part of the structural and weather-management system.

When canopy deck panels deteriorate, leak or are damaged, replacement work should consider how the decking connects with purlins, fascia, gutters, penetrations and lighting. Lake James coordinates redeck work as part of the complete canopy assembly rather than treating it as an isolated sheet-metal task.

Condition Review

Document deck condition and visible framing or drainage issues before defining the work.

Removal & Replacement

Remove affected decking and install replacement material within the approved project scope.

Adjacent Systems

Coordinate fascia, trim, drainage and related repairs needed to complete the canopy envelope.

Do not cover up problems that should be repaired first.

If corrosion or framing concerns are visible below the decking, review structural canopy repair. If water is driving the problem, see canopy drainage repair.
