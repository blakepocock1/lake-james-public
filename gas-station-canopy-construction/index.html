A canopy built around the site—not a generic structure dropped onto it.

Fueling sites have unique clearance, circulation, branding, drainage, lighting and operating requirements. Lake James focuses specifically on canopy work, which means those conditions are part of the planning from the beginning.

Structure

Columns, main beams, purlins, plates and related structural components coordinated for the canopy design.

Envelope

Decking, fascia, soffit, trim, gutters and downspouts integrated with the structural system.

Field Execution

Erection, equipment, access, staging and coordination planned around an active petroleum environment.
