Permanent canopy history

Canopy Passport™

A persistent record for each canopy so operators can see what the asset is, what has happened to it and what should happen next.

A canopy should have a history, not scattered files.

Drawings, photos, inspections, repair quotes and completed work often live in different inboxes or folders. The Canopy Passport keeps the asset identity and history connected to the canopy and location over time.

Asset Identity

Location, canopy type, dimensions, structural system, decking, fascia, drainage and other known specifications.

Inspection History

Baseline and recurring inspection records, photos and condition observations.

Deficiencies & Decisions

Open issues, repair priorities, approved work and deferred items remain tied to the asset.

Project History

Quotes, work orders and completed repair events build a usable maintenance record.

Why asset history matters

When a new manager, contractor or decision-maker looks at a canopy, the most important question is often not just “What do we see today?” but “What has happened here before?” A persistent record reduces repeated investigation and helps the next decision start with more context.

The Passport works with the Canopy Health Score™ and documented inspections to support condition management and capital planning.
