A management tool—not just an inspection sheet

One report. A clearer picture of the canopy.

After sufficient condition information is collected, Lake James Canopies can organize findings into a consistent report that gives decision-makers a practical view of the asset.

0–100 Health Score

A simple overall condition indicator supported by component-level information rather than a stand-alone guess.

Condition Categories

Structure, decking, fascia, drainage, lighting, corrosion, appearance and safety-related observations can be organized in one place.

Priority Findings

Open items are separated by urgency so management can distinguish immediate concerns from planned work.

Photo Documentation

Relevant field photographs support the condition record and make findings easier to communicate internally.

Capital planning

See beyond the next repair call.

The report can organize estimated needs into planning windows so canopy spending can move from reactive emergencies toward a managed capital plan.

Immediate

Items requiring prompt review or action.

0–12 Months

Near-term repair and maintenance priorities.

13–36 Months

Work that can be considered in upcoming operating and capital budgets.

Beyond 36 Months

Longer-range needs that should remain visible and monitored.

The customer deliverable

Built for owners, operators and management teams.

The finished report is intended to communicate the condition story without forcing decision-makers to sort through disconnected photos, repair notes and old quotes.

It can include an executive summary, Canopy Health Score, condition band, priority issues, photographs, recommendations and budget planning windows. The report can then become part of the canopy's ongoing digital record.

Report framework

Executive Summary · Health Score · Component Scores · Priority Findings · Photo Evidence · Recommendations · Immediate Budget · 1–3 Year Outlook · Next Action

From report to action

The report is the beginning of the management cycle.

1. Assess

Collect sufficient field information and document canopy condition.

2. Prioritize

Score, organize and communicate the findings and capital outlook.

3. Execute & Monitor

Move approved work into repair or construction, preserve the history in the Canopy Passport and continue monitoring through Canopy Care™.
