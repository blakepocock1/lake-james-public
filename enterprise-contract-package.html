3. Enterprise SLA & KPI Schedule

Performance is measured from Lake James system timestamps and portfolio records. Resolution deadlines for physical repairs depend on customer approval, parts/material availability, weather, site access, engineering and other dependencies; acknowledgment and management-process targets are separated from physical repair completion.

Priority framework

P1 Critical: apparent immediate safety, structural, operational or major exposure requiring escalation. P2 Priority: material deterioration or operating concern requiring prompt management action. P3 Planned: repair/maintenance suitable for scheduled execution. P4 Monitor: documented condition requiring continued observation.

4. Governance

Customer: executive sponsor, operational contact, approval authority and accounts-payable contact.

Lake James: account lead, operations lead and escalation contact.

Cadence: implementation meetings during mobilization; quarterly Enterprise portfolio review; annual capital-plan presentation where included.

Change control: work outside the SOW requires written scope/price authorization before execution except where an agreed emergency authorization protocol applies.

5. Data & Exit

Customer-specific asset, inspection, deficiency and performance records should remain exportable for the customer. The final MSA should specify ownership of customer data versus Lake James proprietary templates, methods, software and know-how.

At termination, define a transition period, export format, outstanding-work disposition, final invoice process and transfer of customer-owned records so the portfolio is not operationally trapped.
