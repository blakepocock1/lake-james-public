Maintenance is cheaper when it is planned before failure.

Canopies live outdoors over active fueling areas. Water, corrosion, impacts, weather, aging finishes and routine wear all accumulate. A repeatable maintenance process helps operators identify problems earlier and group work logically instead of responding only after a failure becomes visible to customers.
