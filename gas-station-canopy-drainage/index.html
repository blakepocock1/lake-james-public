Water has to leave the canopy somewhere.

When gutters, internal drains, downspouts or discharge paths are undersized, damaged or blocked, water can show up in places it was never intended to go. Lake James evaluates the visible drainage path and repairs or replaces canopy drainage components as part of the overall canopy system.

Internal Drainage

Review concealed drainage paths and visible discharge problems that can contribute to hidden deterioration.

External Gutters

Repair or replace perimeter gutters and downspouts where practical and appropriate.

Related Damage

Address fascia, soffit, corrosion or decking damage associated with chronic water problems.

Drainage problems often reveal larger canopy issues.

Repeated leaks or uncontrolled water can be a symptom rather than the entire problem. A documented inspection can help determine whether drainage repair should be paired with other canopy repairs.
