5-Step Outbound Conversion Playbook

1 · CONTACT

Lead with the management problem, not canopy repair.

Goal: earn a 15-minute conversation.
2 · ROI

Quantify portfolio size, repair spend, emergencies and internal coordination burden.

Goal: establish the economic case.
3 · MEETING

Show the outsourced canopy department model and lifecycle.

Goal: identify 10 representative sites.
4 · PILOT

Assess 10 sites and produce real condition/risk/capital data.

Goal: prove value using their portfolio.
5 · ROLLOUT

Executive review → recommended tier → portfolio proposal.

Goal: paid multi-site enrollment.

Opening message

We help multi-site petroleum operators manage the entire canopy lifecycle—not just repair canopies when something fails. Lake James assesses condition, organizes the asset record, prioritizes deficiencies, plans capital needs, coordinates the work and can execute the repairs. We normally start with 10 representative locations so management can evaluate the system using its own portfolio data before considering a broader rollout.

Discovery questions

How many fuel canopies are you responsible for? · How are canopy conditions documented today? · Who decides which repairs get priority? · Is there a portfolio-level capital forecast for canopy work? · How much internal time is spent coordinating inspections, quotes, vendors and repairs? · What happens when a canopy issue becomes urgent? · Would a 10-location baseline be useful before discussing a larger program?
