TEMPLATE A

Master Services Agreement — Commercial Term Sheet

Parties. Lake James Canopies [LEGAL ENTITY] (“LJC”) and [CUSTOMER LEGAL ENTITY] (“Customer”). Effective Date: [DATE].
Framework. This MSA establishes the general terms governing Statements of Work executed by the parties. No work is required unless described in an executed SOW or separately authorized work order.
Scope separation. Recurring canopy-management services are defined in the applicable SOW. Repair, fabrication, reimaging, replacement, engineering, specialty testing and construction are outside recurring management fees unless expressly stated.
Customer responsibilities. Customer supplies accurate site lists, site contacts, access permissions, known records, timely approvals and safe access. Delays outside LJC control adjust affected dates and service targets.
Fees & invoicing. Recurring fees, project fees, approved expenses, invoicing frequency, payment terms, taxes and price-adjustment terms are stated in the SOW. No customer is activated for recurring billing until commercial authorization is complete.
Change control. Material changes to scope, location count, cadence or deliverables require written change authorization, amended SOW or approved work order before LJC incurs material additional cost.
Records & data. Customer owns Customer-provided records and Customer-specific portfolio outputs. LJC retains ownership of its pre-existing methods, templates, know-how, software and generalized tools. Export/transition requirements should be specified in the SOW.
Confidentiality & security. Each party protects non-public business information using reasonable safeguards and limits use to performance of the agreement, subject to counsel-approved exceptions and required disclosures.
Safety & site conditions. LJC may pause work when site conditions are unsafe. Field/construction work remains subject to applicable safety requirements, site rules, permits and separately authorized scope.
Subcontractors. LJC may use qualified subcontractors where appropriate while remaining responsible for management of its contracted scope. Customer-specific subcontractor restrictions must be identified before launch.
Insurance / indemnity / limitation of liability. [COUNSEL TO INSERT APPROVED LANGUAGE, REQUIRED LIMITS, WAIVERS AND CAPS.]
Term & termination. [INITIAL TERM]. Renewal: [TERM]. Termination for material breach subject to [CURE PERIOD]. Termination for convenience, if offered: [NOTICE]. Accrued approved charges remain payable.
Transition assistance. At termination, LJC will provide agreed Customer-specific portfolio records in a reasonable export format and cooperate in an orderly transition subject to payment of undisputed amounts and any separately agreed transition services.
Disputes / governing law. [COUNSEL TO INSERT NEGOTIATED GOVERNING LAW, VENUE AND DISPUTE PROCESS.]
Order of precedence. Executed change order → applicable SOW/SLA for scope-specific terms → MSA, unless the documents expressly state otherwise.

Priority framework

Critical: apparent immediate safety/structural/operational concern requiring prompt escalation. Priority: material deficiency requiring near-term management action. Planned: repair/maintenance suitable for scheduled budgeting. Monitor: condition retained for future review.

Monthly scorecard: completion %, documentation %, critical/priority acknowledgments, open deficiencies, aging items and quote coordination. Quarterly review: portfolio condition, risk, work completed, upcoming priorities and capital outlook.
