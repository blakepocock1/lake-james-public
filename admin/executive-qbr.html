Executive Quarterly Business Review

One executive review that converts portfolio data into proof of performance, capital priorities, renewal confidence and expansion decisions.

PORTFOLIO CONDITION → WORK COMPLETED → SLA/KPI PERFORMANCE → CAPITAL OUTLOOK → VALUE DELIVERED → NEXT-QUARTER PLAN → RENEWAL / EXPANSION
Portfolio
Condition

Locations, health score, inspections due and critical deficiencies.

Execution
Work

Approved quotes, active work orders, completed priorities and open actions.

Performance
SLA / KPI

Inspection completion, documentation, response and management-review performance.

Finance
Capital

Immediate exposure, 12-month budget and multi-year capital outlook.

Executive QBR Agenda

1. Executive Summary

What changed this quarter, major wins, risks and decisions required.

2. Portfolio Health

Condition trend, critical/priority/planned/monitor items and inspection coverage.

3. Work & Performance

Completed work, open work, SLA/KPI results and exceptions requiring attention.

4. Capital Outlook

Current exposure, next-12-month needs and multi-year funding requirements.

5. Value Delivered

Documented visibility, issues resolved, risks prioritized and management burden reduced.

6. Next Quarter

Top actions, customer decisions, renewal status and justified expansion opportunities.

Management Rules

  • Use actual portfolio records; never invent savings or performance.
  • Separate response-time performance from resolution-time performance.
  • Show trends and exceptions, not vanity metrics.
  • Keep operating work distinct from capital requirements.
  • Every QBR ends with named next actions, owners and decisions.
  • Expansion is recommended only when current service performance supports it.
