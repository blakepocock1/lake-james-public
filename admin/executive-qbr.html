Value Delivered

Document work completed, risk reduced, deficiencies resolved, portfolio visibility improved and management burden coordinated. Use verified operational records only.

Decision Requests

Surface approvals, deferred items, capital decisions, site access blockers and executive escalations instead of burying them in operating detail.

Expansion Case

Use demonstrated performance to recommend additional locations, upgraded management tier or broader capital-planning scope.

QBR Status Workflow

Draft → Ready → Presented → Archived. Each QBR is stored against the customer organization and review period. Renewal health and expansion opportunity are pulled into the executive snapshot rather than re-entered manually.

No revenue, savings, risk reduction or KPI result should be presented as achieved unless supported by verified customer and operating data. Capital estimates remain planning estimates until approved through the normal quote/project process.
