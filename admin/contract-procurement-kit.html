ENTERPRISE COMMERCIAL PACKAGE

Facility Management Contract & Procurement Kit

Commercial framework for moving a qualified customer from proposal approval to contract execution and the 30-day portfolio launch.

PROPOSAL → MSA → SOW → SLA/KPIs → PROCUREMENT CLEARANCE → SIGNATURE → 30-DAY LAUNCH

Legal review required. These are business-term templates and negotiation checklists, not legal advice. Final agreements should be reviewed by qualified counsel and aligned with applicable state law, insurance requirements, project scope and customer procurement terms.

1. Master Services Agreement — MSA

Use one durable agreement to govern the overall relationship while individual SOWs define specific programs and projects.

  • Parties, effective date and term
  • Order of precedence: MSA → SOW → approved change order
  • Payment terms, taxes, disputed invoices and late payments
  • Customer access and cooperation obligations
  • Insurance requirements and certificates
  • Mutual confidentiality
  • Data ownership and permitted use
  • Safety and site rules
  • Subcontractor/vendor use and responsibility
  • Indemnity and limitation-of-liability terms — counsel review
  • Warranty language by applicable work type
  • Force majeure
  • Termination for cause/convenience and cure periods
  • Transition/offboarding and customer-data return
  • Dispute resolution, notices and governing law — counsel review

2. Facility Management SOW

The SOW should make scope measurable and protect Lake James from absorbing construction work into the monthly management fee.

  • Customer portfolio and enrolled locations
  • Selected tier: Essential / Managed / Enterprise
  • Monthly rate per enrolled location
  • Minimum location commitment
  • Contract start and implementation dates
  • Assessment/monitoring cadence
  • Digital asset records and condition scoring
  • Deficiency tracking and prioritization
  • Preventive-maintenance planning
  • Quote/approval coordination
  • Capital forecasting and reporting cadence
  • Portal/dashboard access
  • Emergency coordination scope
  • Customer responsibilities and approvals
  • Explicit exclusions and separately billable work

Always separately billable unless a signed SOW says otherwise: repairs, construction, materials, engineering, specialty testing, extraordinary equipment, major emergency labor and out-of-area travel.

5. Commercial Guardrails

  • Monthly fees pay for the defined management service—not unlimited physical work.
  • No work outside scope without documented authorization except an expressly agreed emergency protocol.
  • Customer-caused delays should stop applicable SLA clocks.
  • Changes in location count, cadence or service tier require written amendment/SOW/change order.
  • Material cost, specialty subcontractors and equipment should remain project-specific unless intentionally bundled.
  • Retain customer portfolio records and an export/offboarding process to prevent data lock-in.
  • Do not promise regulatory compliance or structural adequacy beyond the actual contracted inspection/engineering scope.

6. Signature-to-Launch Handoff

  1. Confirm executed MSA, SOW and SLA schedule.
  2. Verify procurement clearance and insurance.
  3. Confirm customer executive sponsor and operational contacts.
  4. Confirm enrolled location list and tier.
  5. Record effective date and implementation date.
  6. Open the 30-Day Portfolio Launch Command Center.
  7. Import portfolio data and establish baseline.
  8. Complete executive launch review and activate steady-state governance.

Recommended Contract Architecture

MSA: durable legal/commercial framework. SOW: customer-specific scope, enrolled sites, tier, price and exclusions. SLA Schedule: measurable performance definitions and reporting. Change Order / Task Order: separately authorized repair, construction or special project work.

This structure lets Lake James expand or modify work without renegotiating the entire relationship each time.
