What to compare

Five questions to ask before choosing a canopy contractor.

1. Can they diagnose before replacing?

A contractor should be able to separate isolated repair needs from conditions that justify broader replacement or reconstruction.

2. Do they understand petroleum canopies?

Fuel canopies combine structural framing, decking, fascia, soffit, drainage, lighting and the operating constraints of an active petroleum site.

3. Can they handle multiple scopes?

Look for support across structural repairs, decking, fascia, drainage, reimaging, inspections, rebuilds and new canopy construction.

4. Can they support multiple locations?

Multi-site operators benefit from consistent inspections, documentation, deficiency tracking, repair prioritization and capital planning.

5. Will you know what happens next?

Clear project communication, documentation and defined next actions matter as much as the physical work.
